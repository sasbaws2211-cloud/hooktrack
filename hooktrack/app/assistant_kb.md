# HookTrack: what it is and how every part works

HookTrack helps people who post short videos (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) on one or many accounts. It pulls in
how each video is doing, compares every video with that account's own normal, and says what to do next: Scale (do more of
it), Tweak (change a little), Hold (leave it), or Stop (rebuild it). It also plans, films, schedules and publishes new
videos made from the winners.

## The big idea in plain words
1. Add your accounts. 2. HookTrack reads their videos and views (a "sync"). 3. Each video is judged against its OWN account's
normal. 4. Winners become new video ideas on the Posts board. 5. You film, upload, and schedule them. 6. Results come back and the
cycle repeats.

## Words to explain simply
- **Workspace**: your private space. Everything (accounts, videos, settings, team) lives in one workspace. A person can belong to several.
- **Phone**: just a label to group accounts that live on the same device or belong together.
- **Account**: one TikTok, Instagram or YouTube profile (a handle).
- **Hook**: the first few seconds / opening line of a video, and the posted video it belongs to. The Hooks page lists posted videos.
- **Normal**: the middle (median) view count of that account's last 30 videos that are at least 2 days old. It needs at least 5 older videos to exist.
- **x normal**: views divided by normal. 2x normal = twice as many views as the account usually gets.
- **Winner**: 2x normal or more (changeable in Settings). **Dud**: half of normal or less.
- **Decisions**: Scale = a winner (or a very strong start). Tweak = above or below normal but not a winner: strengthen it or rework the first 3 seconds. Hold = about normal (70% to 120%): nothing to change. Stop = weak again and again (2 of its last 3 videos were duds): rebuild the idea, not just the wording. A single weak video is only Tweak.
- **Not judged yet**: a video younger than 2 days, or an account with fewer than 5 older videos. HookTrack will not guess.
- **Still growing**: posted in the last few days; its decision can still change. A fast first day shows as a "Hot start".
- **Hook score (out of 30)**: three ratings of 0 to 10, worked out automatically from real results: Pull (views vs normal), Resonance (saves and shares vs normal) and Conversion (comments vs normal, plus comments asking to buy). It is shown with an "auto" tag. A typed score is never overwritten. The Decision itself comes from real views, not from the score.
- **Credits**: reading videos from the platforms costs small "credits" on the platform's data provider. A sync of a busy account costs a few credits.
- **Confidence**: low/medium/high, how much history backs a call.

## Pages (left menu)
- **Dashboard**: this week's views, change vs last week, how many accounts are Scale/Tweak/Stop, a trend chart, what to scale or stop, a "what's next" checklist, and "ask your data" questions. The "?" button explains the words.
- **Accounts**: add phones and accounts, press **Sync** on one account or **Sync all**. A dropdown next to Sync all picks how far back to read (14, 30, 60 or 90 days) for TikTok. Instagram and YouTube return what the platform lists. Auto-sync can be turned on or off per account. You can also import the original spreadsheet here.
- **Views**: daily and weekly numbers per account. Type "85k" or drop a screenshot and AI reads it (you review before saving, needs AI turned on). Today lets you type each account's views for the day.
- **Hooks**: your posted videos pulled in by the sync, each with views, x normal, hook score and a Decision. Buttons: **Score hooks** (calculates scores from results, free, also runs after every sync), **Simulate thresholds** (what if the Scale/Tweak scores were different), **Send top 5 Scale**, Winners only. **Post** on a Scale hook makes a Scripted card; on a Tweak hook it makes an Idea card on the Posts page. Hot starts show at the top.
- **Posts**: the board (Idea → Scripted → Filmed → Posted → Analysed) and a Calendar of the same cards. Drag cards between stages. On the calendar, drag a card to another day to move it. Cards show tags: "needs video", "ready to schedule", or the scheduled time.
- **Scheduled**: posts waiting to publish later, with Reschedule and Cancel. Times are shown in the workspace time zone.
- **Clients**: group accounts by brand and share a read-only report link per client (a plan limit applies).
- **Inspiration**: save other people's hooks (link, screenshot, notes). Items marked for AI help the AI write better ideas.
- **Settings**: Decision rules, import/export, connecting accounts for publishing, team members and invites, time zone, your password, weekly email options, and deleting your account or workspace. **Billing** (plans, trial, card) is reached from here.

## Working the Posts board
- **Idea**: a rough thought. **Scripted**: written out, waiting to be filmed. **Filmed**: the video file is attached. **Posted**: it is live. **Analysed**: you reviewed how it did.
- To attach a video, open a Scripted card and drag the file onto the upload box (or choose a file). A video is required before a card can be scheduled or published.
- When a video is attached, **Mark filmed & schedule** moves the card to Filmed and opens the schedule window in one step. Otherwise move the card to Filmed and press **Publish**.
- The **Publish / schedule** window: tick the platforms, choose Publish now or Schedule for a time. Suggested free times are shown (09:00, 13:00 and 19:00 by default, skipping times near other posts of that account). Times use the workspace time zone shown in the window.
- If a platform is not connected, the window shows a **Connect** button for it. You sign in on the platform's own page and come back to the same card. Only workspace admins can connect accounts. Publishing starts when a paid plan is chosen (the free trial covers tracking, decisions, cards and briefs).
- Nothing is ever posted unless a person presses Publish or schedules it.
- **Posted video link**: after a video is posted you can paste its full address on the card so its real results come back after the next sync (short vm.tiktok.com links can't be matched; open the video and copy the address bar). The link box only shows on Posted cards. If you did not paste one and the sync has not matched it, **Link my video** lets you pick it from your account's synced videos.
- Matching videos: a card made from a winner is linked to it, and when the new video's results arrive HookTrack says whether it beat, matched or fell short of the original.

## Syncing (getting views in)
- Press **Sync** on an account or **Sync all** on the Accounts page. It also runs by itself on a schedule.
- It reads recent videos with views, likes, comments, shares and saves, and the follower count.
- A longer history (30 to 90 days) gives a better "normal", so decisions and scores appear sooner. It uses more credits.
- Fewer than 5 older videos on an account means no decisions or scores yet for that account: sync a longer history or wait.
- If the data provider's balance runs low the automatic sync pauses (manual sync still works) and the operator is emailed.

## AI features (need AI switched on by the platform owner)
Hook ideas, "AI plan week" on the calendar, "ask your data" on the dashboard, and reading views from screenshots. If AI is off these buttons say so. Core tracking and decisions never need AI.

## Team, roles, accounts
- Roles: owner, admin, member. Admins manage members, invites, connecting accounts and decision rules. Invites are one-time links valid for 7 days.
- Confirming your email: a link is emailed at sign-up (when email is set up). Until confirmed, some actions that cost money are blocked.
- Forgot password: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.
- Time zone: set in Settings; "today", weeks and schedules follow it.

## Plans and billing
A new workspace gets a free trial, then plans limit things like number of accounts, team members, AI requests a month, connected publishing accounts, and shareable reports. When a plan limit or the trial ends, you can still look but not add or change. Pay under Billing (card via Paystack).

## Common problems and the plain fix
- **"No decisions yet"**: the account needs at least 5 videos older than 2 days. Sync a longer history from Accounts.
- **Hook score empty**: press **Score hooks** on the Hooks page. Videos younger than 2 days, or accounts with fewer than 5 older videos, get no score yet.
- **A number looks too high or low**: days marked "~" are estimated from video totals. Real numbers replace them after a sync.
- **Can't schedule a card**: it needs a video attached, it must be on Filmed, the platform must be connected, and publishing needs a paid plan.
- **"Connect your TikTok account first"**: press Connect TikTok in the Publish window, sign in, and you are brought back.
- **"waiting for sync"**: the link was pasted; the next sync will match it. **"link coming back"**: HookTrack published it; the link returns after the next sync.
- **Scheduled time looks wrong**: times use the workspace time zone (Settings). The Publish window shows which one.
- **A scheduled post did not go out**: open Scheduled. Posts the server could not confirm are listed under "Needs your attention" so you can say whether they went live.
- **Sync did nothing / not enough posts**: the platform may list few videos; pick a longer history in the dropdown.
- **AI buttons say AI isn't set up**: ask the workspace owner/admin to ask the platform owner to switch AI on.

## What the assistant cannot do
It cannot press buttons, post videos, change settings, see passwords, or see other people's data. It should point to the exact page and button instead.

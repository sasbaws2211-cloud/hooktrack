HookTrack vs Postiz vs Buffer vs Publer

Four tools, two different jobs. Postiz, Buffer and Publer are built to schedule and publish your posts. HookTrack is built to tell you which of your short videos actually worked, and what to make next. Here is how they compare, including where HookTrack is the weaker choice.

Last checked 20 October 2026 against each company's own pricing and product pages. Prices and features change, so confirm on their sites before you buy.

The short answer

Pick HookTrack if you run several TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts accounts and your real question is "which of my videos worked, and what do I make next?".

Pick Postiz, Buffer or Publer if your main job is scheduling and publishing, especially across many networks, or you want a free plan or a self-hosted tool.

Many teams use a scheduler and a results tool. They answer different questions.

How each one approaches the job

HookTrack

Starts from results. It reads your videos' public numbers for each handle you add, works out each account's "normal" from its last 30 videos, and flags the ones that beat it. Winners turn into new video ideas on a board, and you can schedule the follow-ups.

Trade-off: three platforms only, and no watch-time data.

Postiz

A broad publisher with a composer, per-channel previews, a calendar and repeated posts, plus a public API and an MCP server for AI agents. You can host it yourself because it is open source (AGPL-3.0).

Trade-off: no free cloud plan, and self-hosting needs your own developer app credentials for most networks.

Buffer

A long-running, simple scheduler with a real free plan and a clean queue. Pricing is per channel, which is easy at one to three accounts.

Trade-off: the cost grows with every channel, and TikTok analytics are basic.

Publer

Built for people managing lots of accounts, with per-account pricing and a free plan. Teams add members for collaboration and approvals.

Trade-off: account and member counts both affect the price, so check the total for your setup.

Where HookTrack is the weaker choice

  • You need more than three platforms. HookTrack covers TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. If you also post to LinkedIn, X, Facebook or Pinterest, a scheduler with more channels fits better.
  • You want a free plan. HookTrack has a free trial, then paid plans.
  • You need watch-time or retention numbers. HookTrack reads public numbers, so it does not see how long people watched. Its scores come from views, saves, shares and comments against your own normal.
  • You need a public API or to host it yourself. It has neither today.
  • You want a formal approval workflow for every post. Buffer, Publer and Postiz are stronger there.

Questions

Can I use HookTrack and a scheduler together?

Yes. They do different jobs. A scheduler gets posts out on time. HookTrack tells you which posts worked and what to make next. HookTrack can also schedule to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube itself, so some people use it alone.

Does HookTrack need my TikTok, Instagram or YouTube password?

No. To track results it reads the public numbers for the handles you add. You only connect an account if you want HookTrack to publish to it, and you sign in on the platform's own page.

Is HookTrack cheaper than Postiz, Buffer or Publer?

It depends on how many accounts you have. HookTrack plans start at {{ht_from_price}} a month for 3 accounts. Buffer charges per channel, Postiz by channel bundles, and Publer by account and member. Add up your own accounts before comparing.

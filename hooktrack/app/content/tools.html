Why compare each video with your own normal?

A number of views means different things on different accounts. Comparing a video with your own median shows you which ones really worked, so you can repeat them instead of guessing. Here is how to tell if a TikTok video is a winner.

  1. Paste your numbersCopy views, dates or lengths from your analytics.
  2. We find your normalThe median of your own videos, not a generic benchmark.
  3. See what to repeatEach video gets a clear call: scale, hold or tweak.

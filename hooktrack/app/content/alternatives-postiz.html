Best Postiz alternatives in 2026

Postiz is a strong scheduler: 30+ channels, a calendar, an API and an open-source option. But it is not the right fit for everyone. Here are six alternatives, each with what it is best for and where it falls short, so you can pick the one that matches your actual problem.

Last checked 20 October 2026 against each company's own pages. Confirm current prices on their sites.

Quick picks

  • Want to know which short videos worked? HookTrack.
  • Want free and simple scheduling? Buffer.
  • Managing lots of accounts on a budget? Publer.
  • Want analytics, competitor tracking and ads in one place? Metricool.
  • Only need TikTok analytics or trend research? Exolyt or Virlo.

Why people look for a Postiz alternative

  • There is no free cloud plan. The hosted version starts at $29 a month for 5 channels after a 7-day trial. The free route is self-hosting.
  • Self-hosting takes work. According to Postiz's own docs, most networks need you to set up your own developer app and API keys before you can connect them.
  • You do not need 30+ channels. If you only post short video to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, a focused tool can be simpler.
  • You want answers, not only a calendar. A scheduler gets posts out. Many creators also want to know which posts were worth repeating.

Postiz is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license and has a large community (about 37,000 stars on GitHub). If self-hosting, an API or an AI-agent connection matters to you, it may still be the best choice. See our side-by-side comparison.

The alternatives

1. BEST FOR LEARNING WHAT WORKED

HookTrack

HookTrack reads the public numbers for each TikTok, Instagram and YouTube account you add, works out that account's normal, and tells you which videos beat it. Winners become new video ideas, and you can schedule the follow-ups.

Good
  • Compares each video with its own account's normal, not a generic benchmark
  • Scale, Tweak, Hold and Stop calls with a reason
  • Hook score calculated from results, with no manual scoring
  • Read-only report links for clients

Watch out for
  • Only TikTok, Instagram and YouTube
  • No watch-time or retention data (it reads public numbers)
  • No free plan, no public API, hosted only

2. BEST FREE AND SIMPLE OPTION

Buffer

A long-standing scheduler with a clear queue and a free plan.

Good
  • Free for up to 3 channels, with limits (10 scheduled posts per channel)
  • Paid plans from $5 per channel per month when billed yearly, with a 14-day trial
  • Approval workflows on the Team plan
Watch out for
  • Pricing is per channel, so it adds up with many accounts
  • TikTok analytics are basic
3. BEST FOR MANY ACCOUNTS

Publer

Aimed at people who manage a lot of social accounts, with pricing based on accounts and team members.

Good
  • Has a free plan
  • Per-account pricing, with a free account for every ten (per their pricing page)
  • Extra members add collaboration and approval workflows
Watch out for
  • Both account count and member count change the price, so add up your own total
  • Check which features sit on which plan
4. BEST FOR ANALYTICS, ADS AND COMPETITORS

Metricool

A broader marketing dashboard with scheduling, analytics, competitor tracking and ad reporting, priced per brand.

Good
  • Free plan: 1 brand, up to 20 scheduled posts a month, 5 competitor profiles, 30 days of analytics
  • Starter from about $20 a month billed yearly ($25 billed monthly)
  • Up to 100 competitor profiles on paid plans; approval system on Advanced
Watch out for
  • Pricing is by brand, with more features to learn
  • Free limits are tight for busy accounts
5. BEST FOR TIKTOK ANALYTICS ONLY

Exolyt

A TikTok analytics and social intelligence platform covering brands, audiences, influencers, competitors and trends. As far as its site shows, it is about research and analytics, not scheduling.

6. BEST FOR TREND AND COMPETITOR WATCHING

Virlo

Social listening for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels: track video trends, creators and competitors. Its site advertises a 7-day free trial and focuses on listening and analytics rather than scheduling.

How to choose in one minute

  1. Write down your real problem. "Get posts out on time" and "know what to make next" need different tools.
  2. Count your accounts. Per-channel pricing is cheap at 2 accounts and expensive at 20.
  3. List your platforms. If it is only TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, you do not need 30 channels.
  4. Try two. Most offer a free plan or trial. Run your real accounts through them for a week.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Postiz?

Yes. Buffer has a free plan for up to 3 channels, Publer has a free plan, and Metricool's free plan covers 1 brand. Postiz itself is free if you host it yourself.

What is the best Postiz alternative for TikTok?

For scheduling, Buffer or Publer. For understanding which TikTok videos worked across several accounts, HookTrack. For pure TikTok research, Exolyt.

Can I use more than one of these together?

Yes. Many teams use one scheduler and one results tool. HookTrack can also schedule to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, so you may not need both.

Does HookTrack replace Postiz?

Not for everyone. HookTrack covers three platforms and focuses on results and decisions. Postiz covers far more networks and offers an API and self-hosting. Read the full comparison to see which matches your needs.

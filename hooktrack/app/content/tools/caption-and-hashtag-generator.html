What a good short-video caption does

  • Works as a second hook. The first few words are what people see before they tap "more", so put the point there.
  • Adds context the video cannot. A detail, a question or a source, not a repeat of what was said.
  • Asks for one thing. A question to answer, a video to save, or an account to follow. Not all three.
  • Stays short. Most captions for short videos work best well under the platform's limit.

How to use hashtags

Hashtags help a platform understand what your video is about. They do not guarantee reach, and no number of hashtags will rescue a weak video. A few good ones are better than a long list.

  1. Use a mix. One or two broad tags for the topic and a few specific ones that describe this video.
  2. Keep them relevant. A hashtag that does not match the video can hurt, because it attracts the wrong viewers.
  3. Avoid spammy or banned tags. Platforms can restrict reach for tags they flag. Check a tag in the app before you rely on it.
  4. Look at your own results. If a tag keeps appearing on your best videos, keep it.

Platform guidance on hashtags changes often, so treat any number you read, including ours, as a starting point and check the platform's current advice.

Questions

How many hashtags should I use?

There is no fixed number that works for everyone, and platform advice changes. A few relevant ones, around four to six, is a reasonable place to start. The generator suggests that many, and you can remove any that do not fit.

Do hashtags make a video go viral?

No. They help a platform classify a video, but the video itself decides how far it travels.

Can I use the same caption on TikTok, Reels and Shorts?

You can reuse the idea, but each platform has its own habits and limits, so adjust the wording and the hashtags. Pick the platform in the generator and it will write for it.

Is what I type stored?

No. What you type is sent to our AI provider to write the captions, and we do not store it. We keep only an anonymous count of uses to apply the daily limit.

Do I need to credit AI?

The captions are drafts for you to edit and publish as your own. Check them for accuracy and for anything that does not fit your voice before you post. Some platforms ask you to label AI-generated media, so read each platform's rules.

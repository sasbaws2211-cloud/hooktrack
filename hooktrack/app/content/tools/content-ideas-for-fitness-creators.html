What tends to work in fitness

  • A clear before and after, with a real timeframe, is more convincing than a promise.
  • Show the exercise from the angle that reveals the mistake.
  • Say who the workout is for: a beginner, a busy parent, someone with bad knees.

Questions

Do I need a gym to make fitness content?

No. Home workouts, walking and bodyweight routines are some of the most searched fitness topics, and they are easier for viewers to copy.

Can I give health advice in fitness videos?

Keep it to general information from what you know, say it is not medical advice, and avoid claims about curing or treating conditions. Qualifications matter if you give specific advice.

What makes a fitness hook strong?

A specific mistake or result, such as '3 mistakes ruining your squat', tends to hold attention better than a general 'best exercises' title.

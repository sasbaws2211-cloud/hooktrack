What tends to work in music

  • Start on the most interesting few seconds of sound, not a long intro.
  • Show your hands, your face or the process, not only the audio.
  • Make it easy to react to: a challenge, a mashup or an honest before and after.

Questions

Can I post covers without copyright problems?

Rules differ by platform and country. Many platforms have licences for music in videos, but they can still restrict a video. Check each platform's rules before you rely on it.

How do I get my own music heard?

Use a short, strong clip of your song in a video built around a story or a challenge, not just the song alone.

Do I need professional gear to start?

No. A phone and a quiet room are enough to begin, and many viral clips are rough recordings with a strong idea.

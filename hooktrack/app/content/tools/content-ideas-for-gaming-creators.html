What tends to work in gaming

  • Lead with the surprising moment, then explain it.
  • Name the game in the first line, since viewers search by game.
  • Your reaction is often the content, so keep your voice or face in the clip.

Questions

Can I use game footage without problems?

Rules differ by publisher. Many allow gameplay footage for videos, but some restrict music or monetisation. Check each game's policy.

How do I stand out in a crowded gaming niche?

Pick one game or one style and be the person who knows it best, with a personality viewers can recognise.

Should I use a facecam?

It helps people connect with you, but it is not required. A strong voice and good editing can carry a video.

What makes a good profile bio

A new visitor decides in a few seconds whether to follow. A bio helps them decide if it answers three questions quickly.

  1. Who is it for? Name the person you help: "busy parents", "first-time home buyers", "beginner guitarists".
  2. What do they get? The result or the kind of video: "20-minute home workouts", "one money tip a day".
  3. Why you? Only if it is true and short: a real credential, a number or a personality line. Do not invent one.

Questions

How long should a bio be?

As long as the platform allows and no longer than it needs to be. TikTok's limit is short, so lead with who it is for and what they get. Instagram gives a little more room for a second line.

Should I use emojis in my bio?

One or two can help the eye find the key point, but too many make a bio harder to read. The generator uses at most one per bio.

Should I put hashtags in my bio?

Usually not. Hashtags in a bio take up space, and they do not help people understand what you post. The generator leaves them out.

Is what I type stored?

No. What you type is sent to our AI provider to write the bios, and we do not store it. We keep only an anonymous count of uses to apply the daily limit.

Can I claim results in my bio?

Only ones that are true. The generator is told not to invent credentials or results, but check every line before you publish it.

What counts as a short-form video?

There is no official definition. In practice, short-form video means a vertical video that is short enough to watch in one go on TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts. The platforms have raised their maximum lengths over time, so a short-form video today can be longer than it could a couple of years ago. What matters more than the limit is that the video is only as long as the idea needs.

Video size and aspect ratio for short-form video

The usual choice for short-form video is vertical 9:16, which is 1080 pixels wide and 1920 pixels tall. It fills the screen on all three platforms. Square and other vertical shapes are accepted in some places but can be cropped or padded, and horizontal video is not eligible for YouTube Shorts. If you edit once and post everywhere, export in 9:16 and keep important text away from the edges and the bottom, where each app places its buttons and caption.

How to check a spec yourself

Limits change, and a number that was right last year can be wrong now. Before you rely on one, look it up on the platform's help pages (YouTube Help, the Instagram Help Center, or TikTok Support) or try the upload in the app. If you are about to post the same video on more than one platform, work through the repurposing checklist too.

Questions

How long can a YouTube Short be?

Up to 3 minutes, according to YouTube's help pages (last checked 10 November 2026). Shorts must be square or vertical, and the maximum resolution is 1080p.

How long can a TikTok short video be?

Guides commonly report up to 10 minutes, and some report longer uploads for some accounts. We could not confirm the figure on TikTok's own pages, so check in the app before you plan around it.

How long can an Instagram Reel be?

Newer guides report up to 3 minutes, and older ones say 90 seconds. We could not confirm the current figure on Instagram's own pages, so check in the app.

What is the best aspect ratio for short-form video?

9:16 vertical, at 1080 by 1920 pixels, fills the screen on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Horizontal video is not eligible for Shorts and shows with bars or crops elsewhere.

Does a longer video get more views?

Not by itself. Length that matches the idea tends to work better than length for its own sake. The way to know is to compare your own videos against your own normal.

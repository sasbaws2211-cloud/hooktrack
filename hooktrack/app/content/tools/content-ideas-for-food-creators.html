What tends to work in food

  • Show the finished dish in the first second, then how it is made.
  • Put quantities and times on screen so it can be cooked from the video.
  • Reusable ingredients and a cost on screen make a video feel practical.

Questions

Do I need a fancy kitchen to make food content?

No. A phone, good light and a clean worktop are enough. Overhead shots near a window work well.

Should I include the full recipe in the video?

Put the key amounts on screen and the full recipe in the caption or a pinned comment, so people can save it.

How do I handle dietary and allergy information?

State what a recipe contains, and do not promise it is safe for an allergy or a medical diet unless you are qualified to say so.

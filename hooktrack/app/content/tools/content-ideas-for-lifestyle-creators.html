What tends to work in lifestyle and travel

  • Include real costs and real downsides, not only the highlights.
  • Give one tip someone can use today, even in a personal story.
  • Be specific about place, price and time of year.

Questions

How do I avoid sharing too much of my private life?

Decide in advance what you will not show, such as your home address, your routine in real time and other people's faces, and stick to it.

Do I have to travel to make travel content?

No. Local guides, planning tips and budget breakdowns work from home, and a place you know well is a real advantage.

How do I handle gifted trips and stays?

Say clearly when something was gifted or paid for, and follow the rules of the platform and of the country you are posting in.

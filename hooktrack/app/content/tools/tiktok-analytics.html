How this TikTok analytics checker works

Raw view counts do not tell you much on their own. 40,000 views is a big win on an account that usually gets 5,000, and a flop on one that usually gets 90,000. So the checker works out your normal, which is the median views of the videos you paste, and then shows every video as a multiple of it.

  • Scale: 2× your normal or more. Worth repeating and building on.
  • Hold: somewhere in between. A normal result for your account.
  • Tweak: half your normal or less. One weak video usually calls for a new angle, not a new account strategy.

It uses the median instead of the average because one viral video pulls an average up and makes every other video look worse than it was. The median barely moves.

Where to find your TikTok analytics

TikTok shows your own analytics in the app's creator tools, and on a computer through TikTok Studio. The menu names and locations change from time to time, so if you cannot find them, search TikTok's help for "analytics". Open the list of your videos, copy the view count of each one, and paste them above, one per line. You can add a title before the number, like Morning routine, 18400, to make the results easier to read.

What to look at in TikTok analytics besides views

Views are the easiest number to read and the least informative on their own. Once the checker shows you which videos stood out, open those videos in TikTok's analytics and compare:

  • Saves and shares. They show people found the video useful or worth passing on.
  • Comments. Comments such as "where can I buy this?" signal real interest, not just a quick watch.
  • How long people watched. If TikTok shows it for your account, the first few seconds matter most for the hook.

Give each video a couple of days before you judge it. A video posted this morning is still growing, and judging it early can make a good hook look weak.

What to do with the result

For each Scale video, write down what the first seconds said and how the video was built, then make a close copy with different specifics. For a single Tweak video, change the opening line or the angle and try again. If your last three videos are all duds, the checker will say so, because that pattern usually points to the format rather than one bad post.

Questions

Is this TikTok analytics checker free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you paste are not uploaded or stored.

Does it need my TikTok login?

No. You copy the view counts yourself and paste them in. It never connects to TikTok.

How many videos should I paste?

At least 5, and 10 to 30 gives the most reliable normal. Use videos that have had a few days to collect views.

Why does it use the median and not the average?

One viral video can double an average and make everything else look like a failure. The median is the middle value, so a few outliers do not distort it.

Can I use it for Reels and YouTube Shorts too?

Yes, with the numbers from that platform. There is a version for Instagram and one for YouTube. Check each account on its own, because each platform has its own normal.

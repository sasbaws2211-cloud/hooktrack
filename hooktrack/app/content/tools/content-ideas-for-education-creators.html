What tends to work in education

  • One idea per video, explained so a beginner can follow.
  • Show a worked example or a demo instead of only describing.
  • Cite where a fact comes from when it matters, and correct yourself in public if you are wrong.

Questions

How do I keep education videos from feeling like a lecture?

Start with the surprising answer or the problem, keep sentences short, and change what is on screen every few seconds.

Do I need to be an expert to teach?

You need to be accurate about what you teach and honest about what you do not know. Many good education creators teach what they recently learned.

What is the best length for an educational short?

Often 30 to 60 seconds for one idea. If a topic needs more, split it into a series.

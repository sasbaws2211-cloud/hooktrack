Best time to post on YouTube: find yours

Most advice on the best time to post on YouTube is an average of other people's channels. This free analyzer uses your videos instead: paste the dates, times and views, and it shows which days and hours did best for your channel. It runs in your browser and needs no sign-up or YouTube login.

Why there is no single best time to post on YouTube

A chart that says "post on Thursday at 3 pm" is an average across very different channels, audiences and countries. Your viewers may be students in one time zone, shift workers in another, or people watching on a weekend morning. The only reliable answer is in your own results, which is why this analyzer compares each of your videos with your channel's normal.

How the analyzer finds your best time to post to YouTube

  1. It finds your normal: the median views across the videos you paste.
  2. It shows every video as a multiple of that normal, so a 40,000-view video and a 4,000-view video are judged against your own scale.
  3. It groups your videos by day of the week and by three-hour window, and takes the median of each group.
  4. It ranks the slots that have at least two videos, and tells you how much to trust the answer.

The median is used so one viral video cannot make a single time slot look better than it really is.

How to get your dates and views from YouTube Studio

Open YouTube Studio and go to your content list. For each video, copy the date and time it was published and its view count. Paste one video per line in a format like 2026-10-04 18:30, 4200. Dates such as Oct 4, 2026 6:30 PM work too, as long as the year is included. Layouts change over time, so if you cannot find the publish time, search YouTube's help for it. YouTube Studio may also show when your viewers are on YouTube in its audience reports, if your channel has enough data. It is worth comparing that with your results here.

How many videos do you need?

The analyzer needs at least 8 videos to run, but 20 or more is where the pattern becomes reliable. With 30 to 60 you can see a real pattern. With fewer, a slot can look great because of one lucky video, and the analyzer will tell you when that is the case.

Test your best time properly

  1. Pick your top two or three slots from the results.
  2. For three or four weeks, post the same kind of video at those times.
  3. Compare them with your normal. If they keep beating it, you have found a real pattern. If not, the video matters more than the time, which is common.

Post length and type matter here: keep Shorts and long videos in separate checks, because they usually have very different normals. There is a version for YouTube Shorts. Also available: the best time to post on TikTok and Instagram.

Time zones

The analyzer uses the times exactly as you type them, so use the time zone you plan your posting in. If most of your viewers are in another country, convert the times to your viewers' local time first.

Questions

What is the best time to post on YouTube?

It depends on your audience, and a chart of averages will not tell you. Paste your own videos into the analyzer above to see which days and times did best for your channel.

Is this analyzer free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

Does it connect to my YouTube channel?

No. You copy the dates and views yourself, so it never connects to YouTube.

Does the time of day really change views?

Sometimes, but usually less than the video itself. The analyzer shows whether timing makes a visible difference for your channel, and how sure that answer is.

Should I include Shorts?

Run Shorts and regular videos as two separate checks. They behave differently, so mixing them hides the pattern in both.

What tends to work in beauty

  • Show the result in daylight and in the first seconds.
  • Name the product and the skin type so viewers know if it applies to them.
  • Be honest about what did not work. It builds trust faster than a perfect review.

Questions

Do I need to disclose sponsored beauty videos?

Yes. Most platforms and many countries require a clear label for paid or gifted content. Check the rules where you and your audience live.

How do I avoid making skincare claims I cannot back up?

Describe what you did and what you saw, not what a product will do for everyone, and avoid medical claims about treating conditions.

What lighting works best for beauty videos?

Natural daylight facing a window is the simplest option, and it shows colour more truthfully than a ring light for many looks.

Can you post a TikTok on YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Short vertical videos work on all three platforms, which is why so many creators post one video in several places. The difference between a repost that does well and one that flops is usually small: whether the video was adapted for the new platform, or just copied across with the old platform's watermark, sound and calls to action still in it.

YouTube Shorts vs TikTok: what to change when you repurpose

  • The file. Use the original export, not a downloaded copy with a watermark.
  • The sound. Music from one platform's library may not be cleared on another.
  • The words. References like "duet this" or "link in my TikTok bio" mean nothing on YouTube.
  • The screen layout. Each app covers a different part of the screen with its buttons and caption, so text that was safe on one can be hidden on another.
  • The audience. Viewers on a different platform have not seen your earlier videos, so the first seconds must stand on their own.

The checklist above turns those into specific items. Switch the platforms at the top if you are going from Reels to TikTok, or any other direction.

Don't judge the repost by the original's numbers

The same video can get 40,000 views on TikTok and 3,000 on YouTube Shorts and still be a good result on both, because each account has its own normal. Questions

Can I upload a TikTok video to YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Export the video from your editing app without the TikTok watermark, make sure it is vertical, and upload it straight to YouTube. Then work through the checklist above.

Should I remove the TikTok watermark?

Yes. A watermark shows the video came from another app, and it can look like a recycled repost. It costs nothing to export the original file without it.

Will reposting hurt my reach?

A lazy repost, with a watermark and prompts for another app, can feel out of place and may be shown less. A video that has been adapted for the new platform is a normal post. Whatever the exact rules, the fixes in the checklist are quick.

Can I use the same music?

Not always. Sounds from one app's library may be licensed only for that app. Use your own audio or pick a sound from the platform you are posting on.

How long can a YouTube Short be?

YouTube has changed the maximum length of Shorts over time, so check YouTube's help for the current limit before you upload.

What tends to work in tech and business

  • Lead with the problem the viewer has, not the tool you are showing.
  • Use screen recording so people can follow exactly what you click.
  • Show real numbers when you can, and say when an example is made up.

Questions

Can I give financial advice in business videos?

Share your own experience and general information, avoid promising returns, and say it is not financial advice. Rules differ by country, so check what applies to you.

How do I make technical topics easy to follow?

Start with the result, explain it in everyday words, and show one step at a time on screen.

Do affiliate links need disclosure?

Yes. If you earn from a link, say so clearly. Platforms and many countries require it.

How this Instagram analytics checker works

Instagram shows you numbers, but not whether a number is good for your account. A Reel with 12,000 views is a hit for an account that usually gets 3,000 and a miss for one that usually gets 40,000. The checker takes the median views of the videos you paste as your normal and shows each one as a multiple of it.

  • Scale: 2× your normal or more.
  • Hold: in between, a normal result.
  • Tweak: half your normal or less.

The median is used on purpose. A single Reel that lands in the Explore feed can lift an average for weeks and make your regular Reels look weak by comparison.

Where to find analytics in Instagram

Instagram's own analytics, called Insights, are available on professional accounts (creator or business). Look for the professional dashboard in your profile, then open the content you shared. Copy the views of each Reel, paste them above one per line, and add a short title in front if you like, for example Packing tips, 8200. Menu names change over time, so search Instagram's help for "Insights" if the layout looks different.

What to check in IG analytics besides views

  • Saves. A save often means "I want to come back to this", which is a stronger signal than a view.
  • Shares. Shares to friends and stories are one of the clearest signs a Reel struck a chord.
  • Comments. Questions and buying questions show real interest.
  • Reels and feed posts separately. They behave differently, so keep them in separate checks.

Wait a few days before you judge a new Reel. Early numbers are not final.

What to do with the result

For Reels marked Scale, note the opening line and structure and make a close variation with new specifics. For a one-off Tweak, change the first seconds before you change anything else. If your last three are all Tweak, rethink the format.

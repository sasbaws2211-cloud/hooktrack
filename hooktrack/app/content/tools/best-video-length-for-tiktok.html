Why there is no single best TikTok length

A tip like "keep it under 30 seconds" or "go for a full minute" is an average of very different accounts. A quick comedy sketch, a recipe and a tutorial need different lengths to work. The reliable answer is in your own results, so the analyzer compares each of your TikToks with your account's normal.

How the best-video-length analyzer works

  1. It finds your normal: the median views across the videos you paste.
  2. It shows each video as a multiple of that normal.
  3. It groups your videos into length ranges, such as under 15 seconds, 15 to 30 seconds and 30 to 45 seconds, and takes the median of each range.
  4. It ranks the ranges that have at least two videos, compares the length of your best and weakest videos, and checks whether longer or shorter videos tend to do better for you.

The median is used so one viral video cannot make a whole length range look better than it really is.

How to get your lengths and views from TikTok

Open your TikTok analytics (in the app's creator tools, or TikTok Studio on a computer) and look at your list of videos. For each one, note its length and its view count. Paste one per line in a format like 0:34, 4200, or add a title first: Morning routine, 0:28, 18400. Lengths like 45 (seconds), 1:30 and 1m 30s all work. Menu names change over time, so search TikTok's help if you cannot find them.

How many TikToks do you need?

At least 10 to run the analyzer, and 20 or more for a pattern you can trust. Use videos that have had a few days to collect views. With only a few, a length range can look great because of one lucky video, and the analyzer will say so.

What to do with your answer

  1. Take your best length range and make your next few videos that long.
  2. Keep everything else similar, so you are testing length and not the topic.
  3. After three or four weeks, run the analyzer again. If the pattern holds, keep it. If not, length is not what is holding you back.

If every range looks the same, put your effort into the first seconds of the video instead. A strong hook helps at any length.

Questions

How long should a TikTok be?

As long as the idea needs and no longer. The best length for your account is in your own results, so paste your videos into the analyzer above to see which lengths did best.

Is this analyzer free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

Does it connect to my TikTok account?

No. You copy the lengths and views yourself, so it never connects to TikTok.

Do longer TikToks get more views?

Not by themselves. The analyzer tells you whether longer or shorter videos tended to do better for your account, or whether length does not seem to matter.

What if all my videos are about the same length?

Then there is nothing to compare. Try a few different lengths for a few weeks, and run the analyzer again.

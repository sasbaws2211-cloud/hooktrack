Does timing matter for YouTube Shorts?

It can, but it may matter less than you expect. Shorts are mostly shown to viewers in a feed, so the first seconds of the video often matter more than the minute you published it. The only way to know for your channel is to look at your own results, which is what the analyzer does. If your best and worst slots are close together, that is a useful answer too: it means you can post when it suits you and spend your effort on the hook.

How the analyzer finds your best time to post Shorts

  1. It finds your normal: the median views across the Shorts you paste.
  2. It shows every Short as a multiple of that normal, so a viral one does not drown out the rest.
  3. It groups your Shorts by day of the week and by three-hour window, and takes the median of each group.
  4. It ranks the slots that have at least two Shorts, and tells you how much to trust the answer.

How to get your dates and views for Shorts

Open YouTube Studio, go to your content list, and filter to Shorts. Copy each one's publish date and time and its views. Paste one Short per line, like 2026-10-04 18:30, 4200. Dates like Oct 4, 2026 6:30 PM work too, as long as the year is included. Layouts change over time, so search YouTube's help if you cannot find the publish time.

How many Shorts do you need?

At least 8 to run the analyzer, and 20 or more for a reliable pattern. Shorts are quick to make, so aim for 30 or more if you can. With only a few, a slot can look great because of one lucky Short, and the analyzer will say so.

Test it before you change your schedule

  1. Take your top two or three slots from the results.
  2. Post similar Shorts at those times for three or four weeks.
  3. If they keep beating your normal, keep them. If not, the Short itself matters more than the time.

Keep Shorts and long videos in separate checks. There is a version for regular YouTube videos.

Time zones

The analyzer uses the times exactly as you type them. Use the time zone you plan in, and convert first if most of your viewers are elsewhere.

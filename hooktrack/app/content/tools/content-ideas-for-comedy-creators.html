What tends to work in comedy

  • A situation people instantly recognise beats an original-but-confusing one.
  • Put the funniest beat early and use the rest to build on it.
  • A repeatable character or format makes viewers come back for the next one.

Questions

How do I make comedy videos that are not copies of everyone else?

Use your own specific details: your accent, your job, your family, your town. A situation only you could describe is harder to copy than a trending sound.

How long should a comedy skit be?

Most work best between 10 and 30 seconds. If the joke lands in the first three seconds, you can go slightly longer, but cut every second that does not add to it.

Should I use trending audio for comedy?

It can help a video be found, but the joke has to work without it. Pick a sound that fits the bit, not just one that is popular.

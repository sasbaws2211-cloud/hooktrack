How a short video script is built

Most short videos that hold attention follow the same shape. The script this tool writes uses it too.

  1. Hook (first 1 to 3 seconds). One line that gets to the point and gives a reason to keep watching. You can check any hook with the free hook scorer.
  2. Setup (a few seconds). The problem, the question or the situation, in one or two lines.
  3. Payoff (the middle). The tip, the story or the demo itself, in short beats that each change what is on screen.
  4. Call to action (the end). One clear thing to do next, such as follow, save or comment, and only one.

Tips for getting a better script

  • Be specific about the topic. "3 mistakes beginners make at the gym" gives a far better script than "gym tips".
  • Generate more than once. Use "Write more options" and take the best parts of each.
  • Put it in your own words. Scripts that sound like a person talking do better than ones that sound written.
  • Film what is on screen. Each beat says what to show, so you are not talking to the camera for the whole video.

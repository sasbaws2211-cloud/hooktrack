Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you use HookTrack, why, who else sees it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and specific to what the product really does.

The short version. We collect what is needed to run your workspace and take payment. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising or third-party analytics, and card numbers never touch our servers.

1. Who we are

The operator of this HookTrack service ("we", "us") is responsible for your information. Contact details are available from the operator of this service.

2. What we collect

We do not ask for, and do not store, your TikTok, Instagram or YouTube passwords.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: show your numbers, work out which posts beat their account's normal, run the features you switch on, and keep your workspace secure.
  • To take payment, apply plan limits, renew subscriptions you agreed to renew, and send receipts and payment notices.
  • To protect the service: prevent abuse, fraud and repeat free trials, and limit login attempts.
  • To contact you about your account, security or changes to these documents.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running and securing the service and preventing abuse; your consent where you give it (for example, saving a card for automatic renewal); and legal obligations such as tax and accounting.

5. The one-trial rule

Each person gets one free trial. To enforce that, we keep a normalised form of your email address (lower-case, with any "+tag" removed and, for Gmail, dots ignored) together with which workspace used the trial. We keep this record even if you later delete the workspace or account, because its purpose is to remember that a trial was used. We also refuse sign-ups from known disposable email providers.

6. Cookies

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read it) and Secure on HTTPS. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Your browser may also remember simple preferences on your own device, such as your theme and that you have seen the cookie notice. These stay in your browser and are not sent to us. Because the only cookie we set is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, it does not need your opt-in; the notice tells you about it, and you can bring it back any time from the "Cookie notice" link in the footer. If we ever add optional cookies we will ask for your choice first.

Embedded players. On a winning hook you can press "Play the original here" to watch the original video inside HookTrack. Nothing is loaded from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube until you press play. When you do, your browser contacts that service, which receives your IP address and may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy (YouTube is loaded from its privacy-enhanced domain). We do not control those cookies. "Open on TikTok" and the other links simply open the platform in a new tab.

7. How long we keep it

  • Workspace content is kept while the workspace exists. A lapsed plan or ended trial does not delete it: the workspace becomes view-only. When a workspace owner deletes a workspace, its content is permanently removed.
  • Payment records are kept as long as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute purposes, even after an account is deleted. They are no longer linked to a workspace.
  • Saved card tokens are removed when you remove the card, turn off renewal and remove it, or delete the workspace.
  • Security and rate-limit records are short-lived and cleaned up automatically.
  • The one-trial record is kept as described above.

8. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Provider keys and saved-card tokens are encrypted at rest. Every workspace is isolated from every other. Sessions expire, login attempts are rate limited, and payment webhooks are signature-checked. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your provider keys private.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: you can see your data in the app and export it from the dashboard.
  • Correction: edit your details and workspace content in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your own account at any time in Settings ("Delete my account"). Workspaces where you are the only member are deleted with their content; in shared workspaces you are simply removed, and if you are the only owner you first hand ownership to someone else or choose to delete the workspace for everyone. A workspace owner can also delete a workspace on its own. To ask us to erase other personal information, contact us.
  • Automatic renewal: turn it off or remove your card at any time under Billing.
  • Objection and restriction, and complaints: depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

11. Children

HookTrack is for businesses and adults. It is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top. For material changes we will tell workspace owners by email or in the app. Questions or requests: contact the operator of this service.

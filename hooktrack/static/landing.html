Questions

What counts as a "winner"?

A post that beats the median of its own account's recent mature posts by a set multiple (2× by default). Shorts and long-form YouTube are compared separately, and a post younger than two days is "too early" instead of being judged on a half-finished number.

Do I have to connect my TikTok, Instagram or YouTube login?

No. HookTrack reads the public numbers for the handles you add. To publish, you connect your own publishing provider; HookTrack never asks for your social passwords.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Tracking, winner detection and decisions work without it. AI is optional for reading screenshots, writing hook ideas and planning the week, and you use your own AI key.

How does the free trial work?

You get a free trial with no card needed. When it ends, your workspace becomes view-only until you pick a plan. Nothing is deleted.

Can I cancel or change plan?

Yes. Each payment covers one period, and you can turn automatic renewal off at any time. If you let a plan lapse, your data stays and the workspace becomes view-only until you pay again.

Is my data shared with other customers?

No. Every workspace is isolated: its accounts, posts, views and settings are never visible to another workspace.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a tracker workbook (.xlsx) and your accounts, daily views and hook log come with it. Importing again does not create duplicates.
